# Adrian Hau

### AI systems researcher · Model evaluator · Technical founder

I study how machine-learning systems behave internally — particularly during
training — and build experiments and instrumentation to make those dynamics
measurable, falsifiable, and operationally useful.

I am Co-founder and CTO of **PYPER³**, (https://github.com/PYPER3) where I lead development and research on
**AEGIS**, a training-time observability and governance system.

My work spans model forensics, mechanistic measurement, training dynamics,
instrumental-convergence evaluation, and adversarial testing of AI systems.

---

## Current work

### PYPER³ / AEGIS
Training-time observability and governance for machine learning.

Current work includes replicated training-efficiency studies, optimizer-state
dynamics, distributed fault detection, external validation, and a constrained
trial SDK for shadow-mode evaluation.

### Model forensics
Experimental work on whether model judgments change when value-laden objectives
are introduced into otherwise factual estimation tasks.

Recent work uses within-trajectory controls to distinguish genuine estimate
revision from task-framing effects.

### Training dynamics / mechanistic measurement
Research into recurring internal structure in transformer models, including
depthwise activation patterns and functional attention-head organization.

### StratEval
A structured evaluation framework for instrumental convergence and multi-step
strategic behavior in language models.
